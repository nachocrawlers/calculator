How To Use the RC CoG Calculator

Use a digital corner scale system, such as the SkyRC Bluetooth Corner Scale System, along with a digital caliper to collect the required measurements. Enter all dimensions in millimeters and all weights in grams.

Required Equipment

  • Digital corner scale system
  • Digital caliper
  • Printed Wheel Riser
  • Flat, level setup surface

1. Measure Total Weight

Place the complete vehicle on the scale system and record the total weight.

2. Measure Wheelbase

Use a digital caliper to measure from the center of the rear axle to the center of the front axle. Enter this as the wheelbase.

3. Measure Track Width

Use a digital caliper to measure from the center of the left tire to the center of the right tire at the axle line. Enter this as the track width.

4. Measure Front / Rear Weight

Using the scale system, record the combined front axle weight and combined rear axle weight. The calculator uses this to determine front/rear bias and longitudinal CoG location.

5. Measure Raised Front Weight

Place the wheel risers on the front 2 scales and then tare the scale, Place both front wheels on the wheel risers while keeping the rear wheels on the scales. Chock the wheels so the vehicle cannot roll, then record the new rear axle weight. This is used to calculate vertical CoG height.

6. Measure Left / Right Weight

Record the combined left-side weight and combined right-side weight. The calculator uses this to determine lateral balance and place the CoG crosshair in the top-view graphic.

Tips for Accurate Results

  • Use a flat, level surface.
  • Zero or calibrate the scales before measuring.
  • Let the suspension settle naturally before recording values.
  • Avoid touching the vehicle while the scales are reading.
  • Measure wheelbase and track width carefully with calipers.
